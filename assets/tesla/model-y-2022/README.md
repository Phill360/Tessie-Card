# Original Model Y renders

Downloaded from Tesla on 2026-11-15 and visually checked for the pre-refresh
Model Y body and distinct wheel designs. These are reference assets for the
2022 body style, not a guarantee of every model-year or regional detail.
Tesla retains ownership of its artwork.

| File | Wheels | Tesla wheel option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| white-gemini-19.png | 19-inch Gemini, silver covers | WY19B |
| white-induction-20.png | 20-inch Induction | WY20P |
| white-uberturbine-21.png | 21-inch Uberturbine, red brake calipers | WY21P |

All three are original downloaded PNGs, with white paint (PPSW).
The Uberturbine render has different camera framing from the other two;
align the artwork before adding an in-card wheel picker.
These files are stored locally but are not yet wired into the card or its build.
