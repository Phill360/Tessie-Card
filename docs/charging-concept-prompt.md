## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: preview-only visual design concept for a Home Assistant custom Tessie charging dashboard card.
Create a polished high fidelity UI design board, landscape 1536x1024 composition. Two equally sized tall dashboard cards side by side on a restrained neutral background, left light theme and right dark theme, same content and layout. Small board heading "Tessie Card · Charging concept" and small footer "Design study • Sample data • Vehicle configuration to be confirmed".
Visual direction: precise, quiet, modern Home Assistant energy dashboard; inspired by rendered energy-flow scenes but vehicle-centred and charging-focused. Generous spacing, crisp typography, subtle rounded corners. No phone devices, no browser chrome, no clutter, no cartoon cars.
Each card: top row "Model Y" on left, "Charging" compact green status on right; secondary text "Home · Connected". Main hero is a highly realistic Tesla-app-like studio 3D render of a pearl white Tesla Model Y with dark glass roof and dark wheels, front-left three-quarter perspective, nose pointing toward right, correct automotive proportions, grounded soft shadow, isolated within the card. It must be a realistic vehicle render, not an icon. Small wall charger on left with a tasteful thin green path running toward the vehicle rear charging port, labelled "AC charging" and "7.2 kW". Do not show solar panels or home batteries.
Below hero: large "62%" and smaller "286 km" with label "Estimated range". Horizontal green battery level bar with a visible target marker at 80%, label "Charge limit 80%". Do not imply bar is full at 62%.
Below: two columns "Time remaining" / "1 h 45 min" and "Ready by" / "16:35".
Below: quiet 3-column information strip "Added" "12.4 kWh"; "Current" "32 A"; "Voltage" "230 V".
Bottom: labelled charge-limit slider with "80%" value, slider track 50–100%, thumb at 80%. Full-width quiet filled action "Stop charging", small secondary action "Charging settings".
Same sample data both cards. Text must be legible, exact labels, no invented metrics, no duplicated controls. Light uses white surfaces and charcoal text; dark uses charcoal surfaces and soft white text. Both use restrained green for active charging only. Professional product design presentation.
